# AI-tell lists

- `kobak_excess_words.csv`: the 900 excess words of Kobak et al. (2025),
  "Delving into LLM-assisted writing in biomedical publications through
  excess vocabulary", Science Advances 11(27), eadt3813, copied unchanged from
  https://github.com/berenslab/llm-excess-vocab (`results/excess_words.csv`,
  MIT License, Copyright (c) the authors). `counting/ai_tells_test.py` uses the
  words annotated `style`.
- The Graphite tell sets (Druck, Paredes and Smith 2026, "AI Tells",
  https://graphite.io/five-percent/research/ai-tells, accessed 2026-09-28) are
  written into `counting/ai_tells_test.py`, quoted from the article's figure
  notes and tables.
